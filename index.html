        About this data and sources
        • Revenue: NIGC FY2025 Gross Gaming Revenue Report (July 2026) and prior NIGC annual releases. FY2019–FY2023 totals are as rounded by NIGC.
        • The “$25M–$250M” concentration row is derived by subtracting NIGC's published top and bottom brackets from 100%.
        • Litigation and policy: compiled by Pechanga.net and checked against Mick Bransfield's Prediction Market Litigation Tracker (mickbransfield.com), court dockets and court reporting. Statuses change quickly; the tracker is reviewed every morning and changes are published only when confirmed by a court record or two independent reports.
        • Tribes In The News: Pechanga.net stories from the last 30 days. Tribes are identified by matching each story's headline and summary against a list of federally recognized tribes, their common short names and their casino brands (for example, Foxwoods for the Mashantucket Pequot). Each story is tagged with up to two issues using keyword rules. Tagging is automated and lightly reviewed, so occasional misses or extra matches are possible. Duplicate headlines are counted once.
        • Map outlines: U.S. Census Bureau boundaries via the us-atlas project.
